📲 How to Install the APK on Your Android Device

Installing an app outside the Play Store (side-loading) takes less than 30 seconds:

1

Download APK

Tap either Download LaynRider or Download LaynDriver button above on your Android phone.

2

Open Downloaded File

When download finishes, tap the notification or open your phone's Downloads folder and tap the .apk file.

3

Allow from This Source

If prompted with "Install unknown apps", tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source" for Chrome/Files.

4

Tap Install & Launch

Tap Install. Once complete, tap Open and log in with your email to start booking or driving!
